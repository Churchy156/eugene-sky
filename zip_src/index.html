Notes & limits

Weather history: Open-Meteo ERA5 reanalysis (1950→ ~5 days ago) + forecast model for recent/future days. Space weather and markets are global (from the Eugene dataset, updated every 3 h). Fatal crashes: NHTSA FARS by county 1975–latest release (~18-month lag). Power: EAGLE-I county history 2014–2025; no free nationwide live outage API (poweroutage.us/ODIN), so live status = utility outage-map links. Police/911: only cities with open daily datasets (Seattle, SF, LA, Chicago, NYC, Cincinnati, Austin, New Orleans). ER visits and most fire/EMS logs aren't public. Effects are simple averages, not causal.
