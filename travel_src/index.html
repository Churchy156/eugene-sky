🗺️ Itinerary

Flight times are local airport times. Airports: type a 3-letter code (EUG) or a city. Hotels: a city, or a street address. Drives: pick "arrive by" or "leave at"; "leave at" is local time where you start, "arrive by" is local time at the destination. Drives over about 10 hours get suggested overnight stops.

Data: Open-Meteo forecast, ERA5 archive & air quality (CC BY 4.0) · NWS alerts · FAA NAS status · NOAA SWPC · OurAirports · OSM Nominatim · Drives: OSRM routing, Photon place names, ODOT TripCheck / CHP / state work-zone feeds via our relay (no live traffic speeds). Results are cached in your browser to respect free rate limits. Forecasts reach 16 days; later dates show 20-year typical weather.
